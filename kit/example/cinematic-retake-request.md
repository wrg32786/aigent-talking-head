Why: the previous take repeated a word ("build, build") and the camera angle felt off. The retake should have better camera and body movement.

Prompt: seated in a leather chair, dusk high-rise office, slow dolly push-in from medium to medium close-up with a gentle arc to his left, eye level, no cuts; leans in on "Want to go further?", opens hands on "build your own AI business", counts on "ninety days", sweeps on "a staff of AI employees", points at camera on "Comment AWAKEN" and holds. Dialogue said once each, no repeated words.

Result: single "build". Speech blocks 0-1.01, 1.66-2.84, 3.12-5.17, 5.59-6.69, 7.04-9.66, 10.36-11.46, 11.92-12.53. faster-whisper word times ran 0.3-0.6 s early on this take; captions were timed from the silence map.
