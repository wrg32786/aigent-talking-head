# aigent-talking-head v1.22.1

Two fixes from our first live test of Studio with a terminal agent.

## What is fixed

- **Connect agent closes when you are connected.** A moment after your agent starts listening, the sheet closes and the chat is ready to use. Escape always closes it too.
- **Your terminal answers faster.** While your agent listens to Studio, a message you type in the terminal now waits at most 30 seconds, down from up to 2 minutes.

## Update

Tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and restart Studio".
