# aigent-talking-head v1.12.0

Rendering the captions, titles and graphics is about 5 times faster, with the same frames as before.

## What is new

- **Faster renders.** Chrome now turns each overlay frame into an image itself, on several threads, instead of taking a screenshot of the page every frame. On a 30 second reel with titles behind the head and screen cards, that step went from 163 s to 31 s, and render plus assemble from 206 s to 74 s. Measured on a cloud computer with 8 threads and no graphics card; with a graphics card it should be at least as fast.
- **Same picture.** All 2160 frames of that reel matched the old frames pixel for pixel, and the finished reels match.
- **Safe fallback.** If your Chrome cannot use the fast way, or it fails part way through, the render carries on the old way and tells you. Nothing to set up.
- **Fix: captions over a screen recording.** A caption next to a screen recording card is now moved clear of it, instead of sitting on top of the recording.

The idea comes from [motlab](https://github.com/staryone/motlab) (MIT). Details and numbers: [docs/rendering.md](https://github.com/wrg32786/aigent-talking-head/blob/v1.12.0/docs/rendering.md).
