# aigent-talking-head v1.29.0

## Asset Vault for B-roll

Knowing what B-roll to use, and where to get it, was the hard part. This release gives every B-roll slot a plain answer to "what should this show?" and gives you one place to keep everything you can show.

### Each slot says what it should show

- Every B-roll slot now has a short brief made from your own words under it. The card reads like "Show OmniRoute on screen. You name it in this line."
- We look for the tools, sites, names and numbers you say first, then plain things you mention. A tool you name asks for a screen recording of it. A number asks for a graphic.
- What the reference showed at that moment is only a hint for the kind of shot. Your reel is about your subject.
- If you work with a connected agent, it can rewrite a brief when it can say it better.

### The Vault

- **Vault** is a new button in the top bar. It opens from any step, and each slot card has **Open the Vault** too.
- Put in your clips, photos, screenshots and logos, and paste links to your site, your profiles and your product pages. We keep it all on your computer and every reel can use it.
- For a link we open the page in a clean browser with no sign in and save its share image, the top of the page, the full page and a short scrolling recording.
- Find things with four filters (kind, source, tag, used or not) and a search box that reads names, tags, brands, page titles and the words on a saved page.
- Every item has a rights mark: Mine, Licensed, Public page or Reference only. A link to someone else's video or post is saved as Reference only. It can guide you, and it is never placed in a reel.
- The same file or link twice is added once. Delete has Undo for 7 days.
- Your old B-roll library moves into the Vault by itself the first time you open it. Same clips, same credits.

### Picks with a reason

- For each slot we look in your Vault first and show the best match with one line that says why, such as: From your Vault: says "OmniRoute" on screen.
- **Use it** places it. **Swap** shows the next ones. **Skip** leaves you on screen. **More** has Upload and the buttons for your agent.
- A strong match is placed for you after a draft and says "Picked for you". A weak one is only suggested.
- With nothing in your Vault, and only if you added a free stock key, the card offers stock. Nothing is fetched until you press Use it. Generating still asks first and shows the price.
- The same item is never picked for two slots in one reel.

### Private by default

- The Vault never leaves your computer. We open a link only when you add it.
- Your agent gets words about each item. Pictures stay with you unless you turn on "Let my agent see Vault pictures" in Settings.
- Your agent cannot delete anything from the Vault.

### Coming later

This release matches by words. Still to come: finding things by what a picture shows (a small model that runs on your computer), spotting near copies, recognising you in your own clips, downloading your own videos from social sites, folders we watch for new files, refreshing old captures, capturing pages you need to sign in to, and made up clips as a last resort in the picking order.

## Vault fixes from our review

- Picks are matched on whole words now. A clip called "postcard" is no longer picked for a line about your car.
- "B-roll picks itself" and Suggest no longer fail on a draft where a slot has nothing in your Vault.
- If Studio is closed while your old B-roll library moves in, the next start carries on where it stopped. No names, tags or credits are lost.
- Undo works when you deleted a link while we were still saving it.
- A link that could not be saved the first time is tried again when you paste it again.
- An upload the Vault turns down (a full Vault, a file that is not a video or a picture) no longer leaves a hidden copy on your disk.
- The B-roll step stays quick when your Vault holds many saved pages.
- Only the page can delete from the Vault, on every route.
