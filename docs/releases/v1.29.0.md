# aigent-talking-head v1.29.0

Titles like the reference. When a reel you like sets every piece of text as a designed phrase, your reel now gets designed phrases too, on your own words, with the same build and the same way out. And B-roll gets a home: the Asset Vault keeps your own files and links, and each empty spot says what it should show and suggests the best match from your Vault.

## Titles like the reference

- **Phrase titles, not one-word captions.** We read the reference's text as whole titles: one to three lines, small words in white and one bigger key word in colour. A draft puts one title on each matching phrase of what you say, in the reference's lines and look. If the reference has no running captions, your draft has none either.
- **Your words, never theirs.** A title starts on its first word, and no word shows before you say it. Titles sit above your head or across your chest, never on your face.
- **The last word is not left alone.** We now look ahead when we split what you say into phrases, so a long sentence does not end with one stranded word.

## Read the titles with my agent

- The text reader often misreads designed titles. On the reference's breakdown there is one small card: **Send to my agent** when your agent is connected, **Copy for my agent** always. Your agent looks at pictures of the reference and sends back each title as it was designed.
- Your agent reads how a title is set (its lines, key word and look) apart from how it moves (how it builds, its effect, how it leaves). Where it can see a title and cannot read it, our own read of that title stays. We never make up words.
- What your agent read stays. Reading the reference again keeps it.
- If your chat uses Anthropic or OpenAI with your key, you can let that model read the pictures instead. It only happens when you click, and the button says who gets the pictures.

## Title effects and exits

Six new ways for a title to arrive, each with a preview in the effects menu:

- **Type.** Letter by letter behind a caret, line after line.
- **Slot roll.** Digits roll and lock left to right, or one word of a line swaps through a list while the rest stays.
- **Count up.** A number counts up and can roll to a final text, such as 1 Day to 365 Days to 1 Year.
- **Stacked lines.** New lines stack and older ones go grey, blur and fade.
- **Decode.** Random letters settle into the key word.
- **Flap board.** The title flips into place like a station board.

Four ways for a title to leave: **blow up** past the lens into a flash, shrink into a tilted **polaroid** that slides off, **glitch slice**, and **dip to black**. When a title ends on a cut, its exit is the transition at that cut: the picture makes the same move, and the sound there belongs to the exit.

In Studio, Text and graphics has one small group, Title effect and exit: pick one of each for the reel. Glow and dim now work on a single title too. If your words cannot carry an effect (a count needs a number you say), the title comes in the plain way and the build log says why.

## A subtitle file with every reel

Every render now writes `reel-vN.srt` beside the reel: what you say, in short cues, ready to upload with the video. It is written even when the captions are already in the picture.

## The check before you post looks at the picture

- **Frozen or black picture.** The check now measures the finished reel. A picture that freezes for half a second or goes black where the edit did not ask for it fails the check. Fades, dips to black, cards and the hold at the end are ours and do not count.
- **Notes, not failures.** Flicker, clipped whites or blacks, a soft picture and extra cuts are listed as notes for you to look at.
- **Every cut as a pair.** We compare the frame before each cut with the frame after. A cut that hardly changes the picture, or a jump in colour nobody planned, is listed with both frames. This never blocks a reel.

## Asset Vault for B-roll

Knowing what B-roll to use, and where to get it, was the hard part. This release gives every B-roll slot a plain answer to "what should this show?" and gives you one place to keep everything you can show.

### Each slot says what it should show

- Every B-roll slot now has a short brief made from your own words under it. The card reads like "Show OmniRoute on screen. You name it in this line."
- We look for the tools, sites, names and numbers you say first, then plain things you mention. A tool you name asks for a screen recording of it. A number asks for a graphic.
- What the reference showed at that moment is only a hint for the kind of shot. Your reel is about your subject.
- If you work with a connected agent, it can rewrite a brief when it can say it better.

### The Vault

- **Vault** is a new button in the top bar. It opens from any step, and each slot card has **Open the Vault** too.
- Put in your clips, photos, screenshots and logos, and paste links to your site, your profiles and your product pages. We keep it all on your computer and every reel can use it.
- For a link we open the page in a clean browser with no sign in and save its share image, the top of the page, the full page and a short scrolling recording.
- Find things with four filters (kind, source, tag, used or not) and a search box that reads names, tags, brands, page titles and the words on a saved page.
- Every item has a rights mark: Mine, Licensed, Public page or Reference only. A link to someone else's video or post is saved as Reference only. It can guide you, and it is never placed in a reel.
- The same file or link twice is added once. Delete has Undo for 7 days.
- Your old B-roll library moves into the Vault by itself the first time you open it. Same clips, same credits.

### Picks with a reason

- For each slot we look in your Vault first and show the best match with one line that says why, such as: From your Vault: says "OmniRoute" on screen.
- **Use it** places it. **Swap** shows the next ones. **Skip** leaves you on screen. **More** has Upload and the buttons for your agent.
- A strong match is placed for you after a draft and says "Picked for you". A weak one is only suggested.
- With nothing in your Vault, and only if you added a free stock key, the card offers stock. Nothing is fetched until you press Use it. Generating still asks first and shows the price.
- The same item is never picked for two slots in one reel.

### Private by default

- The Vault never leaves your computer. We open a link only when you add it.
- Your agent gets words about each item. Pictures stay with you unless you turn on "Let my agent see Vault pictures" in Settings.
- Your agent cannot delete anything from the Vault.

## Vault fixes from our review

- Picks are matched on whole words now. A clip called "postcard" is no longer picked for a line about your car.
- "B-roll picks itself" and Suggest no longer fail on a draft where a slot has nothing in your Vault.
- If Studio is closed while your old B-roll library moves in, the next start carries on where it stopped. No names, tags or credits are lost.
- Undo works when you deleted a link while we were still saving it.
- A link that could not be saved the first time is tried again when you paste it again.
- An upload the Vault turns down (a full Vault, a file that is not a video or a picture) no longer leaves a hidden copy on your disk.
- The B-roll step stays quick when your Vault holds many saved pages.
- Only the page can delete from the Vault, on every route.

## Smaller fixes

- **A title your agent cannot read is not lost.** When your agent marks a title as unreadable, the draft keeps our own read of that title. Before, that title went missing.
- **A slash in a title stays.** A title such as "OPEN 24/7" stays on one line. Only a slash with a space on each side starts a new line.
- **Titles for speech in any alphabet.** Phrase titles now work when you speak Russian, Greek or any other script.
- **The check does not fail a pause you asked for.** A reel with effects may hold the picture on purpose. The check now lists that as a note.
- **Keep my look means your look.** Start a new video (or drop in new footage) with Keep my look after a Recreate, Copy the look or Influence, and the new video gets your own look back, plus anything you picked after the reference. Before, it carried the reference's picks into the next video.
- The picture descriptions in the README and the Studio guide now use today's step names.
