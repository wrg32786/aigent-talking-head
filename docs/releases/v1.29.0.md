# aigent-talking-head v1.29.0

Titles like the reference. When a reel you like sets every piece of text as a designed phrase, your reel now gets designed phrases too, on your own words, with the same build and the same way out.

## Titles like the reference

- **Phrase titles, not one-word captions.** We read the reference's text as whole titles: one to three lines, small words in white and one bigger key word in colour. A draft puts one title on each matching phrase of what you say, in the reference's lines and look. If the reference has no running captions, your draft has none either.
- **Your words, never theirs.** A title starts on its first word, and no word shows before you say it. Titles sit above your head or across your chest, never on your face.
- **The last word is not left alone.** We now look ahead when we split what you say into phrases, so a long sentence does not end with one stranded word.

## Read the titles with my agent

- The text reader often misreads designed titles. On the reference's breakdown there is one small card: **Send to my agent** when your agent is connected, **Copy for my agent** always. Your agent looks at pictures of the reference and sends back each title as it was designed.
- Your agent reads how a title is set (its lines, key word and look) apart from how it moves (how it builds, its effect, how it leaves). A title it can see and cannot read is left out. We never make up words.
- What your agent read stays. Reading the reference again keeps it.
- If your chat uses Anthropic or OpenAI with your key, you can let that model read the pictures instead. It only happens when you click, and the button says who gets the pictures.

## Title effects and exits

Six new ways for a title to arrive, each with a preview in the effects menu:

- **Type.** Letter by letter behind a caret, line after line.
- **Slot roll.** Digits roll and lock left to right, or one word of a line swaps through a list while the rest stays.
- **Count up.** A number counts up and can roll to a final text, such as 1 Day to 365 Days to 1 Year.
- **Stacked lines.** New lines stack and older ones go grey, blur and fade.
- **Decode.** Random letters settle into the key word.
- **Flap board.** The title flips into place like a station board.

Four ways for a title to leave: **blow up** past the lens into a flash, shrink into a tilted **polaroid** that slides off, **glitch slice**, and **dip to black**. When a title ends on a cut, its exit is the transition at that cut: the picture makes the same move, and the sound there belongs to the exit.

In Studio, Text and graphics has one small group, Title effect and exit: pick one of each for the reel. Glow and dim now work on a single title too. If your words cannot carry an effect (a count needs a number you say), the title comes in the plain way and the build log says why.

## A subtitle file with every reel

Every render now writes `reel-vN.srt` beside the reel: what you say, in short cues, ready to upload with the video. It is written even when the captions are already in the picture.

## The check before you post looks at the picture

- **Frozen or black picture.** The check now measures the finished reel. A picture that freezes for half a second or goes black where the edit did not ask for it fails the check. Fades, dips to black, cards and the hold at the end are ours and do not count.
- **Notes, not failures.** Flicker, clipped whites or blacks, a soft picture and extra cuts are listed as notes for you to look at.
- **Every cut as a pair.** We compare the frame before each cut with the frame after. A cut that hardly changes the picture, or a jump in colour nobody planned, is listed with both frames. This never blocks a reel.
