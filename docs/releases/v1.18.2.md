# aigent-talking-head v1.18.2

Recreate now drafts from the pieces you kept.

## What is fixed

- **Your pieces, not the whole take.** With a reference on Recreate, Draft used to go back to your whole take, so a line you had cut out came back.
- **Render comes on after the draft.** Finish kept saying "Your pieces changed since the draft" right after you drafted, so Render never turned on. Now the draft counts as done and Render is next.

- **Pick a reference first, then add your take.** Adding your first take no longer clears the reference you just picked.
