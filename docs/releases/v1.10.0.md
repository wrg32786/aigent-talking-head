# aigent-talking-head v1.10.0

Post your reel straight from Studio. A new last step sends the finished reel to your Buffer account, saved as drafts so nothing goes out until you say so.

## What is new

- **The Post step.** After Render, pick your Buffer channels (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, X, Facebook Reels, Threads), write one caption and send. Each channel gets its own post.
- **Drafts first.** Posts land as Buffer drafts. You can also add them to your queue or pick a time. Post now is there too, but only after you tick "Yes, publish this now".
- **Your video, hosted where you choose.** Buffer fetches the video from a public link, so Studio uploads it to your Cloudflare R2 bucket or your Cloudinary account, or you paste a link you host. Dropbox and Google Drive links do not work with Buffer, and Studio tells you so.
- **No surprises per platform.** Studio checks the video first. If the reel is too long for Facebook Reels (90 s) or YouTube Shorts (3 min), that channel is skipped with the reason and the rest still go. Captions over a platform's limit are caught, and videos are converted to the format Buffer wants.
- **Less on screen.** The step shows one small part at a time: Connect, Channels, Send. Extra options per channel are folded away.
- **Your keys stay yours.** Your Buffer, R2 and Cloudinary keys stay on your computer and only Studio's local server uses them.
- **Your agent can post too.** The new `buffer_post_reel` tool sends a reel to Buffer as drafts. `buffer_channels` lists your channels and their limits.

## Update

In your copy of the kit: `git pull`, then `npm run setup:all` (it only installs what is missing). Or tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and run npm run setup:all". Then open Studio, go to the Post step and follow Connect. The guide is in [docs/posting.md](https://github.com/wrg32786/aigent-talking-head/blob/v1.10.0/docs/posting.md).
