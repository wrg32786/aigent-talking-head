# aigent-talking-head v1.22.0

When the reference has a graphic or effect Studio does not, Studio makes it for you.

## What is new

- **Made for your menu.** A sticker, a styled text label or an effect like a light leak in the reference is made as a small HTML and CSS scene and added to the menu with a REF badge, under "Made from your references". It stays there for every later job.
- **Your words, its look.** A label made from the reference shows a word you say at that moment, never the reference's word.
- **Its sticker, not a placeholder.** The reference's own sticker image is used when the breakdown saved it.
- **Your agent can make one too.** Ask your agent for an effect the menu lacks; it shows up on the Look step without a reload.
- **See what Recreate brings.** The reference card lists its shots, framing, cutaway spots, sounds and whether it has music, plus what was made for your menu.
- **Its timer and number titles.** On Recreate, a timer or number title in the reference gets a title from your own matching words, like "five minutes".

## What is fixed

- **No music when the reference has none.** Recreate now leaves the music bed off for a reference with no music.
- **Graphics at the right moments.** On Recreate, the reference's graphics move with your re-cut length instead of keeping the reference's times.

## Update

Tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and restart Studio".
