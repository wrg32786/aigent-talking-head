# aigent-talking-head v1.18.1

Uploading a new video in Studio starts a fresh edit, and you can clear all B-roll from the timeline.

## What is new

- **A new upload starts a fresh edit.** When you drop or choose a new talking-head file on a board that already has an edit, Studio clears the cuts, pieces, B-roll, reference and draft. The look resets too, unless you chose **Keep my look** in **New video**. Files are never deleted. The last reel stays in Outputs, and **Open a past video** still opens your other jobs.
- **The leftover B-roll is gone.** The B-roll step was still showing spots from the last video after you uploaded a new one, because it kept reading the old draft on disk. That leftover draft is now ignored until you draft again.
- **Clear all B-roll.** On the B-roll step, **Clear all B-roll** takes every clip off the timeline (it asks first). **Undo** puts them back. Your library is unchanged.
- **Your agent can do it too.** New tool `clear_broll`. `fill_slot` with `clear` and `all` does the same.

## Update

Tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and restart Studio".
