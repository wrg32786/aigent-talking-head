# aigent-talking-head v1.6.0

Captions and titles stay off your face, and the cut-out that puts words behind your head is cleaner, steadier and fits on a 16 GB machine.

## What is new

- **No more text on your face.** The kit finds your face in every part of the reel and keeps captions, titles and kinetic words off your eyes, nose, mouth and chin. A word that would land there moves up onto your hair or down below your chin, or gets a little smaller. Words behind your head stay behind. The render tells you how many moments still had text on your face, and the check fails if any did.
- **A better cut-out.** Words behind your head now use BiRefNet portrait, the model made for people, one step below the slowest one. Hair keeps its strands and the edge does not flicker.
- **Faster rebuilds.** Only the parts with words behind you get cut out, most frames are filled in from their neighbours, and every frame is saved, so changing a caption and rendering again takes seconds instead of minutes.
- **Fits in 16 GB.** The cut-out uses about 8 GB at its peak. Before it starts, the kit checks free memory. If there is not enough, Studio says so and lets you put titles in front, use the lighter cut-out, or try anyway.

## Update

In your copy of the kit: `git pull`, then `npm run setup:all` (it downloads the new cut-out model, about 928 MB, once). Or tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and run npm run setup:all".
