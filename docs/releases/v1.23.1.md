# aigent-talking-head v1.23.1

Recreate now follows the reference more faithfully.

## What is fixed

- **Music stays when the reference has music.** Before, a reference with music could come out with no music at all when we had no matching track. Now we pick one of ours.
- **Your reel keeps its own look.** Recreate no longer swaps in our default brand or adds glitch transitions the reference never had.
- **Clean cuts.** Where Recreate re-cuts your take, it is a hard cut, never a transition in the middle of a sentence.
- **Forbid works on everything.** An effect or graphic made from the reference that you forbid stays out of the draft.
- **Effects land on the cut.** Flashes and light leaks made from the reference line up with your cuts.
- **A tidier menu.** One label style shows once, not once per position.
