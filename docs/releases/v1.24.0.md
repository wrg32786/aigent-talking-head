# aigent-talking-head v1.24.0

Recreate now copies the reference's effects, camera moves and colours.

## What is new

- **The same transitions.** Flashes, whips, zooms, dissolves, wipes and the rest carry over cut by cut, in the direction the reference used.
- **The same camera.** A slow push-in, pull-out or drift inside a shot, and a shake where the reference was handheld.
- **Effects made for you.** When the reference uses something we did not have, like a spin, we make it as a REF scene and put it on that cut.
- **Studio looks closer by itself.** With a Gemini or OpenAI key in Settings, Studio asks a vision model to describe each shot, so the effects it names (a light leak, confetti) are made too. Your key stays on your computer.
- **The reference's colours.** Your titles, stickers and cards take the reference's accent colour, on the closest of our themes.
- **The Effects switch works.** In Influence, turning Effects off now leaves out the effects made from the reference.
