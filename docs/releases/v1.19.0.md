# aigent-talking-head v1.19.0

Recreate now re-cuts your take to match the reference.

## What is new

- **Punchy cuts like the reference.** With a reference on Recreate, Draft cuts your one take (or your kept pieces) to the reference's shot plan, scaled to your length. Every cut sits in the quiet between two words, so no word is ever cut, and your voice runs straight through.
- **The framing changes on every cut.** Each shot gets the reference's framing as a punch in or out: close-ups closer, medium shots wider.
- **Its sounds, at its times.** The pops, dings and clicks in the reference play at the same moments in yours, using our own sounds.
- **No music when the reference has none.** A reference with quiet or no music now leaves the bed off.
- **Its boxes and stickers.** Boxes and stickers the reference drew become graphics slots you can fill.
- **Titles that do the same job.** Your opening claim, a number, a time or a brand name takes the style of the reference title doing that job.

## What is fixed

- **A line you dropped stays dropped.** Setting a cut's end in a pause no longer pulls the next line back in.
- **Word times match the sound.** A transcript that runs a little early or late is moved onto the audio.
