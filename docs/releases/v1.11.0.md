# aigent-talking-head v1.11.0

Start fresh with a new video in Studio, and go back to an old one, without losing anything.

## What is new

- **New video.** Press the **+** next to the reel name, or **More**, New video. Name the next reel and press **Start fresh**. You land on Footage with a clean board: no footage, cuts, reference, b-roll, draft or chat from the last one.
- **Keep my look.** On by default, so the new reel starts with the same pack, style picks, music, sound, pace, export size and brand logos. Turn it off to start from the defaults.
- **Nothing is deleted.** The last reel's footage, renders and saved edits stay on disk, and Studio saves where you were.
- **Open a past video.** **More**, Open a past video lists your reels with the date and how many reels each has. Pick one and you are back where you left it. Older jobs open with their footage and reels, so **Edit this reel** still works.
- **Safe to press.** Studio will not switch while a draft, render, download or recording is still running, and tells you what to wait for.
- **Your agent can do it too.** New tools `new_video`, `open_video` and `list_videos`. Tell it "start a new video called spring-launch".

Tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and restart Studio".
