# aigent-talking-head v1.4.1

A fix release from a long Studio session on Windows. Clips keep their first and last words, several files play in the order you gave them, files you already cut are used as they are, and a 13 minute recording shows its lines in seconds instead of after every clip is encoded. Captions spell your brand words your way, and a brand's logo can pop up when you say it.

## What is fixed

- **Clips keep their first and last words.** A clip never starts after its first word begins, and it holds a little longer after the voice so a soft ending like the s in "this" is not clipped. A tiny leftover piece joins the line before it instead of becoming its own clip.
- **Several files play in your order.** Different files (not takes of one script) keep the order you gave them. Files you already cut are used whole, in order, with no re-cut.
- **Retakes and fragments.** A line is only called a retake when most of it matches another line. Very short or cut-off lines are marked as fragments and left out of the draft.
- **Long recordings.** The list of lines is ready as soon as the speech is split, clips encode in parallel (on your graphics card when it has an encoder), progress shows as it goes, and the contact sheet is split into pages, which fixes "The filename or extension is too long" on Windows.
- **Chat changes are real.** Asking your agent to reorder or drop footage now does it, or says why not. A change that does nothing says so in the Studio chat, so your agent cannot tell you it changed something it did not.
- **One music bed.** The bed in the draft is the bed the music panel shows. None means no music.
- **Instagram references.** When instaloader is missing or Instagram refuses it, yt-dlp fetches the post anonymously, and a failure shows the real reasons. Nothing logs in.
- **Tools found after install.** Studio now uses the kit's own Python, where the installer put yt-dlp and instaloader. An **Install** button next to "not installed" fixes it from the page.
- **A finished render stays finished.** Pressing cancel just as a render finishes keeps the video.
- **Gentler tightening.** A tightened pause keeps a short breath, and the Choices table lists every pause it cut with **Keep this pause**.

## What is new

- **Your words.** Style, Words and brands: write a word the way Whisper hears it and the way you write it ("Agent Academy = AIgent Academy"). It is fixed in captions, titles and the clip list and keeps its own capitals, even in an all-caps caption style. AIgent Academy, Skool, claude.ai, ChatGPT and Claude Code come built in.
- **Keyword logos.** Add a logo for a word and it shows on a small white plate when you say it. Only logos you add, or the kit's own Skool mark.

## Update

In your copy of the kit: `git pull`, then `npm run setup:all` (it only installs what is missing). Or tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head".
