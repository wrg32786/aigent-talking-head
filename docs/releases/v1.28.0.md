# aigent-talking-head v1.28.0

Recreate gets much closer to the reference, there is a new way to use one (Copy the look), and Studio is laid out in eight clear steps with a Cut step you can read at a glance.

## Recreate matches the shot, not just the cut

- **Your framing follows theirs.** For every shot we measure where the reference's face sits (how big, how high) and crop your take to match. A wide take filmed sideways now comes out as the same medium close-up as the reference. We stop zooming before your footage goes soft and tell you when a shot hit that limit.
- **Your colour follows theirs.** We match your take's colour to the reference shot by shot and keep skin natural, so a cold room can sit in a warm reel without an orange face. Your finish (grain, vignette and the rest) still goes on top.
- **Starburst stickers.** When the reference puts a spiky star behind the head with FIRST, SECOND, THIRD on it, we now draw a star with the same number of points and colour, behind your head, with the word in italic serif. The round seal is still there for reels that use one.

## Copy the look (new)

Recreate is the same edit: their timeline on your take. Copy the look is the same style on your own script and subject.

- Save any reference's style as a named look and use it again on any take, in any project, with no reference attached.
- All timing comes from your words. Cuts land in your own pauses at the look's pace and never inside a word.
- The look brings the framing, colour, caption style, title style, stickers on your own key words, sounds, transitions, camera moves and music choice. Nothing from the reference's script or text appears.

## How close is it

After a Recreate or Copy the look draft we write a short read-out: framing, colour, captions, font, stickers, sounds and pace, each with a score and one plain line. It is an estimate made before rendering, and it says so.

## Studio: eight steps

Studio now walks you through eight steps: Start, Cut, Picture, Text and graphics, B-roll, Music and sounds, Review, Render. What you see comes first, sound has its own step, and the last two check the edit and make the video. Old links still open the right place.

- **Start.** Under your footage you choose what guides the edit: Just my footage, Recreate a video, or Copy the look. Copy the look can also use a look you saved before, with no reference at all.
- **Save as a look.** On Copy the look, one button saves the reference's style under a name so you can use it on any later take.
- **How close is it, on Review.** A row of chips says Matched, Close or Off for framing, colour, captions, graphics, sounds and pace, and Matched, Close or Guess for the font.
- **Review, then Render.** Review plans the reel and shows what we picked. Render makes the video once, at full quality. Post is a view inside Render.

## The Cut step, made clear

The Cut step now shows three layers, in order, each with one line that says what it is:

1. **Raw take.** Your take in full. Play it, or click a word to jump there.
2. **Best lines.** Every line, sorted by what it does (hook, story, tip and so on). The ones we recommend are marked Best, and each one plays.
3. **Clips.** Our best lines stitched together into the full edit and the short clips, with the quiet trimmed. Play each one as it will sound and tick the ones to keep. The clips you keep are what moves on.

The page now uses three words only: raw take, lines and clips.

- **One Pacing control.** Natural, Tight or Very tight says how much quiet comes out. The stitched previews change right away, and the draft uses the same choice. We only trim quiet between words. We never cut a word and never start in the middle of one.
- **What to make** (format, length, full edit or clips, organic or ad) is one folded row.

## What's the point of this video?

One optional line at the top of Cut, for example "Show beginners they can build an app with AI in a weekend". It is also offered on Start when you pick Copy the look.

- It steers which lines we mark Best, the Hook, Story and Tip picks, the full edit and the clips. Picks it lifted say **Fits your point**.
- In a draft, a word from your point becomes the big word of a caption or title when you say it, and Copy the look puts its stickers on your ordinals, numbers and point words before any other word.
- Your connected agent gets the same line. It is saved with the project and cleared when you start a new video.

## Topics that mean something

The topic chips on Cut used to be single weak words like "help", "back" or "able". They are now up to five real themes from your take, such as "starting with ChatGPT" or "building with AI". Click one to see only its lines. Your agent or a model names them when one is connected; otherwise we find them on your computer. When nothing good comes out, the row stays hidden.

## See where a breakdown is

When we break down a reference video, one line under the progress bar now says what is happening: Downloading, Finding cuts, Reading captions, Reading camera moves, Transcribing, Reading music and sounds, Reading graphics, Reading framing, Reading colours, Building the recipe. The bar follows the real stages. A small folded list shows every stage with a check as each one finishes. Reload the page and it shows the same place. If a stage stops, we say which one and offer Try again.

The line also says how long the stage has been going and counts up ("Reading graphics, 1 min 20 s"), so you can see it is working. Each finished stage keeps its time in the list.

## Words never sit on top of each other

In a Recreate, a big title word could land on a small caption word (a "Terms" on an "on"). A caption word used to stay on screen until the next spoken word, even when a title came in first.

- A word that has been said now leaves when a title comes in. One word leaves before the next one enters.
- We check every piece of text against every other on every frame. If two would touch, one moves above or below the other with a gap, or waits its turn.
- Text still stays off your face and inside the safe area, in 9:16, 4:5 and square.
- The check runs on every render, and the reel does not pass if two words share a spot.

## The font comes from your reference

A Recreate could use the font combo you picked for your last video. It now uses the reference's style.

- When we break down a reference we read the font of the words on screen: what kind it is (sans, serif, condensed, blackletter), how heavy, upper or lower case, and its colour. We match it to the nearest font we ship and draw your captions and titles with that.
- On Review, a chip says **Font: Matched**, **Close** or **Guess**. Matched means the letters look the same. Close means the same kind of font. Guess means nothing we have is near, and we used the nearest.
- With Recreate or Copy the look, a font you picked before choosing the reference is cleared. Pick a font on Text and graphics after that and yours is used instead. Back to my look gives your earlier font back.
- A new reference or a new video starts with no font picked, so an old pick never rides along.
- We only use fonts that come with the kit. They are all free to use (SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts), and nothing is downloaded. One new pairing, `anton`, covers tall narrow type.

## Nothing from your last video in a Recreate

A Recreate could come out in the look of the video before it. Studio kept your last picks on the board (caption style, title entrances, transitions, colour, effects, music, sounds, pace) and sent them with the draft, where they went over the reference.

- When you choose Recreate or Copy the look, the board is set from the reference alone. Your earlier look is put aside, not lost: Back to my look brings it back.
- What you pick after that is yours and is used. That holds while a link is still being read too.
- A new reference starts clean again.
- If your earlier look is in use and the reference's is not (the reference is switched off, or on Influence over your own look), the reference card says so: "Your saved look is in use, not the reference's". One button, Use the reference's look, switches, and Undo puts your look back.
- Clips made on their own get the reference's look too.
- B-roll ideas from an older draft or an older reference are dropped when you draft again, so no search runs on old words. Clips you placed and ideas from your agent stay.
- A board you made with an older version is fixed the first time Studio opens it.

## A breakdown no longer stalls on Windows

Our Python now reads and writes every file as UTF-8, so a reference with a non-English character in it goes through.

## Titles look like the reference's

In a Recreate the reference had two-font titles (a small line in capitals over one big word in an italic serif, glowing on a darkened picture) and you got one plain word. Now we read the look of the titles and draw it.

- **Two lines when the reference has two.** The rest of the phrase is drawn small with the key word, in the same places and cases as the reference.
- **The two fonts.** We read whether the key word is a serif or a sans, upright or italic, and the same for the small line, and pick the pairing we ship with those two faces. The colours come across too.
- **Glow and dim.** When the reference's key words glow, ours do. When its picture goes darker behind a title, ours does. You can also turn them on yourself (`title-glow` and `title-dim` in a Reel.md).
- **The blur entrance.** When we cannot read how a title came in, an italic serif key word now comes in from a blur. Every entrance stays open to you on a rebuild.

## Nothing under a title

A caption word could sit under a title, or flash next to it for a frame or two.

- While a title is up, the caption bar shows nothing. A word that started before the title leaves when the title starts.
- A fast phrase no longer pushes its last word into the next title. The words before it give up a little time instead.
- When a title moves to land on the beat, we say so in the build log, one line per title, and its caption word moves with it. Add `"snap": false` to a title to keep it where you put it.
- The check before you hand over a reel now looks at every frame round each title's start and end, on the frames we really drew. It fails if a word is there.

## Text we are not sure of

- A label that only repeats the caption word is not drawn any more.
- When we are not sure we read a title in the reference right, we mark it in the report and in Studio's breakdown under "Check this text", with a field to fix it right there before a draft.
- A caption word that is hard to read against the picture now gets a soft shadow by itself, even in a style with no shadow, and the build log says which words.

## B-roll spots show up

A Recreate used to drop the reference's B-roll when we had no clip for it. Now every cutaway in the reference becomes a B-roll spot in the draft, with the reference's effects on it (push, shake, glitch, colour, split screen or behind the head, and the way in and out). When we have no clip, the B-roll step shows a **Needs B-roll** card that says what the clip should show, with Upload, Use stock (when you have a key set), Copy for my agent, and Skip. Your edit says how many spots still need a clip.

## Calmer Studio

Every step in the step bar has its name. The bottom bar no longer covers the page. The top of Cut is slimmer, What to make sits on Clips, and the tiles, look names and Post wording are plainer.

## Small things from the live Recreate

- **No line across your head in the split.** The soft shadow under the split panel used to run over your forehead. It now falls on the room behind you and your head stays clean.
- **A website fits in the split panel.** A wide clip, such as a website, is now shown whole: sharp, sitting on the seam, over a soft blurred copy of itself, with your head rising in front of it. Clips that are closer to square still fill the panel. An insert can ask for either with `"fit": "whole"` or `"fit": "cover"`.
- **No stroke on a title's small words unless you want one.** The small words of a title used to get a dark outline every time. They now follow your caption outline: with no outline, they are drawn clean.
- **Easier to read is your call.** Text and graphics has one switch, "Make hard-to-read words easier to read". It puts a soft shadow behind a word that is hard to read against the picture. It is on by default, and starts off when your reference has no outline or shadow on its captions.
- **Close the chat.** The chat has a Close button at every width. The chat button in the top bar brings it back, and Studio remembers your choice.
- **Phrases are not one-word captions.** When a reference shows whole phrases on screen, we no longer call them one-word captions. The recipe uses the kinetic style, and the report says why. One title per phrase comes in 1.29.0.
- **We say when the vision pass was off.** If a reference was read with no vision model, the reference card says "We read this reference without the vision pass, so titles may be misread", and so does the report.

## Camera moves you can see

A reference's slow push in is often only 3 to 7 percent. On a talking head nobody sees that. We now measure how big each move in the reference is and scale it so it shows on your take. On Picture, under Motion, **Camera moves** has three sizes: Subtle, Medium and Strong. A Recreate starts at Medium. Change it and render again, no new draft needed.

## A clean first frame and a clean last word

A take often runs on for a word after your closing line ("So what are you waiting for? Like,"). The last piece now ends with the sentence. And when there is quiet before a piece's first word, the piece starts on the first frame you look at the lens, so it no longer opens on you looking down. We only take out quiet. No word is cut.

## Fixed along the way

- **Copy the look on a close-up take.** A take that is already as close as the reference is no longer pushed in further, and whenever we do zoom we keep the whole head in frame.
- **Stickers land on better words.** No more stickers on generic nouns. When a take has few strong words, we place fewer stickers.
- **No more "install the Effects pass" when it is already installed.** When part of a breakdown stopped, the page blamed the Effects pass. Now it says the part did not finish and offers Read it again. We only ask you to install something that is really missing.
- **Music and sounds** no longer has a second pace control. It shows the Pacing in one line and points to Cut.

## A version for the Instagram Feed

Instagram's Feed refuses a 9:16 video ("only eligible for Instagram Stories"). So a render can now make more than one size at once:

- **Reels and Stories, 9:16.** The reel you already get.
- **Instagram Feed, 4:5 (1080x1350).** Saved as `reel-v1-feed-4x5.mp4`.
- **Square, 1:1.** Saved as `reel-v1-square-1x1.mp4`.

The 4:5 and square versions are made again, not cropped. The frame follows your face and keeps room over your head. It holds still while you do and moves gently at a cut. Captions, titles, stickers, cards and B-roll are laid out again inside the new shape and kept off your face. Your words and timing stay exactly the same.

In Studio, tick the sizes you want on the Render step. Outputs says what each file is for. The Post step tells you which file goes where and picks the 4:5 for an Instagram Feed post by itself. If you have not made it yet, one button makes it. Buffer posts are still saved as drafts.

## B-roll that does not go missing

A Recreate on a reference full of B-roll used to come back with none. We read the reference's framed screen panels as graphics, not as B-roll, and when there was no clip to hand we quietly placed nothing.

Now a breakdown finds each B-roll moment in the reference: where it sits (a framed panel under the speaker, a full cutaway, a split screen), what it showed, how it moves, and how it comes in and goes out. Recreate keeps a slot at each of those moments, landed on your own words. Copy the look keeps the same number a minute and the same kinds, on your lines that fit.

A slot with no clip is never dropped. The B-roll step shows one card for it: "Needs B-roll" and what it should show, with Upload, Use stock (when you have added a key; keys stay on your computer), Send to my agent or Copy for my agent (a ready instruction to capture it) and Skip. Put a clip on a slot and it sits, moves and arrives the way the reference's did. A panel never covers your face. Where a slot is still empty when you render, you stay on screen.

The step itself is calmer: an idea that waits for you looks like an idea, and the keys and the other sources are tucked away until you ask for them.

The step now starts by counting: "6 of 19 B-roll spots filled". So you can see at a glance what your reference has and what is still empty.

### Fast run

Some reels flash through a handful of pictures in a second. We call that a fast run, and you can now make one. On the B-roll step choose Fast run, pick two or more clips, pictures or sites from your library in the order they should play, and press Make the run. We cut them on the beat of your music and place them as one piece of B-roll. Each one is shown whole, sharp over a soft blurred copy of itself, with a short push in. Paste a public link under Add a site and we capture it for you, so showing thirty of your sites takes a few clicks. Where your reference cuts fast, its card asks for a fast run.

A wide clip in the split panel is also shown whole now. Before, we cropped it to its middle and the sides were lost.

Knowing what B-roll to use and how to capture it is the next step. The design is in `docs/specs/broll-sourcing.md`.

## For agents and the command line

- `npm run reel -- ... --export reels,feed45,square` makes every size in one run. `npm run export -- <job> --export feed45` makes the 4:5 of a finished reel.

- `npm run look -- save <teardown dir> --name <name>`, `npm run look -- list`
- `npm run reel -- --style-look <name or path> --clip <take>`
- `npm run reel -- --goal "<the point of the video>"` steers caption emphasis and sticker words. Studio passes your point for you.
- MCP: `get_edit`, `get_cuts` and `get_pieces` return `point`; lines that serve it carry `fits`. Each reference in `studio_get_reference_teardown` has `stages` (the current stage, a percent and the list). No new tools.
- `--motion subtle|medium|strong` sizes the reference's camera moves (default medium; the Style line `motion`; works with `--rebuild`). A teardown writes `move-size` on a shot line. Studio: `pace.motion`.
- `cut.json` inserts: `"run": [files]` with `"step"` makes a fast run on the bed's beats; `"fit": "whole" | "fill"` on any insert. MCP: `add_broll` with `run` and `step`; `list_broll` returns `slots` (total and filled) and `need.run` on a fast run slot.
- `--keep-ends` leaves the two ends of every piece as cut (by default the last piece ends with its closing sentence and a piece starts at the lens; `cut.json` `edges` lists what moved).
- `--no-reframe` and `--no-grade-match` turn the two matches off; so do the Reel.md Style lines `reframe: off` and `grade-match: off`.
- New files in a draft: `build/match.json` (with a `font` part), `build/textcheck.json`, `build/grade*.cube`. A teardown's `extras.json` gains `font`, and its Reel.md the Style lines `font`, `font-face` and `font-match`.
- `npm run verify` fails when two words share a spot on any frame. `--fonts <pairing>` still puts your own font over a reference's.
- `build/plan.json` gains `mode`, `look` and per-shot `face`, `crop` and `grade`.
