# aigent-talking-head v1.10.1

Fixes from a real 10 minute session: long silences that tightening missed, a stray noise you could not reach, a reel that could not be rebuilt after a new draft, and a logo that stopped the render.

## What is new

- **Silences found by listening.** Tightening now finds pauses in the audio, not in the transcript, so a word the transcript stretched over a silence no longer hides it. On a 5.5 minute test take, 1.9.0 left 10 pauses over 0.6 s in the tightened draft. Now it is none, and still no cut lands inside a word.
- **Clean clip ends.** A clip ends where your voice falls away, then leaves a short breath.
- **Noises in pauses.** A short click or cough between two lines is cut along with the pause. You or your agent can also cut any stretch out of a clip by hand (`npm run cut`, or the `cut_range` tool), and `npm run cut -- noises <job>` lists the short sounds it found.
- **Edit a finished reel.** Every reel keeps the edit behind it. A new draft no longer loses it: press **Edit this reel**, or run `npm run reopen -- <job> v3`.
- **Logos just work.** SVG and PNG logo files no longer stop the render.
- **Clearer Draft and Render.** After a draft Studio says "Plan ready, no video yet. Press Render." If you kept cuts, it offers to render them.
- **Better reference reads.** Black frames and very short shots no longer set the finish. Studio tells you when it used the rougher built-in reader and installs rea-video in one click. Influence borrows a reference's picks; Recreate copies its look.
- **"AIgent" reads right** in small caption faces.
- **Same clips, same titles.** The lock `title-words=one` or `title-words=phrase` pins whether titles are one word or a phrase.
- **Check your own footage.** `python3 kit/studio/tx_chunks.py take.mp4 take.tx.json && node kit/cut-bench.mjs take.mp4 --words take.tx.json` now also counts long pauses left in.

## Update

In your copy of the kit: `git pull`, then `npm run setup:all` (it only installs what is missing, and now offers rea-video). Or tell your agent "Update aigent-talking-head and run npm run setup:all".
