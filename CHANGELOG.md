## 1.29.0 (unreleased)

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.29.0.md](docs/releases/v1.29.0.md).

Titles like the reference (Studio report 4, items 16 and 17; the reference 5 text teardown).

- **Phrase titles from the reference's text tracks.** `kit/teardown/phrases.cjs` groups `shots[].text.items[]` (the big text rea-video samples every half second) into one title per phrase: its lines (1 to 3), the key word (the line in a colour, or set clearly bigger), band and height, start and end, how it builds (`word`, `letter`, `line`), the effect the samples show (a count gives `count-up` with from, to and unit; a swapped last word gives `slot-roll` with the words; older lines going grey gives `stack-fade`; typing gives `type`), how it leaves (`blow-up` into a flash cut, `dip-black` into a fade), and whether it starts on a cut, ends on one or stays across one. Small print, handles, dark print on cards and lone words are left out. `kit/videomd2reel.cjs` writes them when the text on screen is phrases: Reel.md title lines gain `lines`, `key`, `build`, `effect`, `exit`, `swap`, `count`, `band`, `glow`, `on-cut`, `across-cut` (read back and checked by `kit/reelmd.cjs`), `titles.json` carries the same, the report has a Phrase titles table, and stickers or app labels that were pieces of a phrase read apart are dropped. The Style line `running-captions: off` is written when the reference has no running captions. New constants in `kit/rules.cjs`: `TITLE_EFFECTS`, `TITLE_EXITS`, `TITLE_BUILDS`.
- **The pattern on your own words.** `kit/phrase-titles.cjs` `mapPhrases` splits your speech into phrases (pauses, punctuation, at most seven words, never a lone word) and puts one title on the phrase nearest each reference title's moment, scaled to your length: your words in the reference's number of lines, the key word on its own line when the reference's is, its build, effect and exit. A title starts on its first word and carries every word's time, so no word shows before it is said; it sits above the head or across the chest, never on the face; `snap: false` keeps beat sync from moving it. `count-up` is kept only on a number you say, `slot-roll` only on a number, an exit that is a transition only where you cut. `kit/reel.cjs` uses it for a Recreate (`--recipe ... --recut`) and for Copy the look (the saved look keeps the pattern in `titles.phrases` with none of the reference's words, and `captions.running: false`), and sets `runningCaptions: false` in `captions.js` so the draft draws no one-word captions under phrase titles. Tests: `tests/phrase-titles.test.mjs` (21).
- **Read the titles with my agent.** A teardown writes contact sheets of the stretches with big text (`<teardown>/title-sheets/`, `kit/sheets.mjs` `titleStretches`, `writeTitleSheets`, `kit/sheets.py titles`) and a ready instruction (`titlesInstruction`, `ask.txt`). Studio's breakdown has one card (`RefTitles.jsx`): Send to my agent when one is connected, Copy for my agent always, and Read them with Anthropic or OpenAI only on its own click, naming who gets the pictures. The agent answers with the new MCP tool `set_reference_titles` (schema checked, mirrored by `POST /api/reference/titles`, `kit/studio/ref-titles.mjs`); the titles are kept in `titles.agent.json` beside the teardown, win over the OCR read and stay through a re-run. Tests: `tests/studio-ref-titles.test.mjs`, `tests/studio-ref-titles.e2e.mjs`.
- **Title effects engine.** `kit/overlay.html` gains a phrase title renderer (`phrase()`, `phLay()`): the lines as written, small partner words and one larger key word in the accent, each word on its own time (`"words"`), never drawn before it is said. Six effects, one per title (`"effect"`, or `"titleEffect"` for the job): `type` (letter by letter with a caret at about 12 letters a second, line after line), `slot-roll` (the key word's digits roll with motion blur and lock left to right; with `"swap"` one word rolls through a list while the rest of the line stays), `count-up` (`"count": { from, to, unit, final }`, an eased count that can roll to a final text), `stack-fade` (new lines stack, older ones go grey, blur and fade), `decode` (seeded random glyphs settle left to right) and `split-flap` (the title on a flap board). Four exits (`"exit"`, `"titleExit"`): `blow-up`, `polaroid`, `glitch-slice`, `dip-black`. `"glow"` and `"dim"` now work on one title in a job that has them off. Every frame is drawn from the time alone (seeded glyphs, no state between frames), so the same moment drawn twice is the same picture.
- **A title's exit is the transition.** When a title with an exit ends within 0.2 s of a cut, `kit/rules.cjs` `titleExitSeams` gives the picture's move there and `kit/assemble.cjs` uses it in place of the segment's own transition (the build log says which it replaced): `polaroid` and `glitch-slice` are new drawn xfade expressions in `kit/xfx.cjs` that move the picture by the same numbers the overlay moves the title, `dip-black` is a fade through black, `blow-up` is a hard cut under the flash. Part of the move plays before the cut (`lead`), under the title as it leaves.
- **One table for title names.** `kit/title-effects.json` has one row per entrance, effect and exit: what it needs (a number, a swap list, two lines, a cut), what is drawn when it cannot be, and for an exit the xfade, length, lead and sound. `kit/rules.cjs` and `kit/reelmd.cjs` read their lists from it (`TITLE_TABLE`, `titleRow`), and `npm run check` fails when `kit/overlay.html` names something the table does not. When a draft from a reference cannot give an effect what it needs, `mapPhrases` returns `fallbacks` and `npm run reel` prints one line each (which effect was not drawn and why). With `sfxMap` on, a title exit gives the sound at its seam from its row, in place of the transition's whoosh (`kit/sfx-map.cjs` `autoHits(cut, flips, caps)`).
- **Effect and exit for the whole reel.** `--title-effect <name>` and `--title-exit <name>` (`contract.json`), the Style lines `title-effect` and `title-exit` in a Reel.md, and in Studio two single picks, Title effect and exit (one each), in their own group on the Text layer (`kit/studio/categories.json`, `KIND_MODE`), refused with titles off like the entrances. `list_effects` with `titles` lists the effects and exits after the entrances. `docs/effects.md` gains "Title effects and exits" with previews made by `npm run menu:render -- --only title-effects`; the menu, the showcase and the coverage checks include them.
- **The vision pass reads how a title is set apart from how it moves.** `set_reference_titles` and `POST /api/reference/titles` also take `{ start, end, set: { lines, key, font, glow }, move: { build, effect, exit, swap, count } }` and `{ start, end, unread: true }` for a title the agent saw and could not read (its span is kept in `titles.agent.json` and our own read of that moment stays in the recipe, never guessed). The instruction states the exact number of sheets and asks for a result, not a list of options. Every phrase title in `titles.json` says `from` (`ocr` or `agent`) and `state` (`proposed` or `fixed`).
- **Phrases with a look-ahead, and an .srt beside every reel.** `kit/phrase-group.cjs` `group` splits speech at sentence ends and pauses and picks the split with the fewest phrases that each last long enough and read no faster than 30 letters a second; a stretch nothing fits is marked `forced`. `kit/phrase-titles.cjs` `ourPhrases` uses it, so the last word of a long stretch is not left alone. Every render writes `reel-vN.srt` from `captions.js` (`toSrt`: 2 lines of about 42 letters, 1 to 7 s a cue), also when the captions are burned in or not drawn.
- **Verify measures the picture.** `kit/picturecheck.cjs` runs `freezedetect`, `blackdetect`, `signalstats`, `blurdetect` and a scene count on the finished reel. A freeze of 0.5 s or black outside what the edit does on purpose (from `cut.json` and `captions.js`: the fade in, dips, cards, inserts, the tail, a padded segment) is a FAIL line; flicker, clipping, a soft picture and more hard cuts than planned are notes. Our own changes to the pack's numbers: freeze is read at 540 px wide (at 270 a still speaker reads as frozen), clipping uses the 10th and 90th percentile (our captions put pure black and white in every frame), and the cut count only speaks up for more cuts than planned.
- **Verify looks at every cut as a pair.** `kit/joins.cjs` compares the last frame before each cut with the first after: a hard cut that hardly changes the picture is a `break`, a colour jump with the same finish and grade on both sides is a `drift`. Both are notes with the two frames (`verify/join-*.png`), written to `verify/joins.json` with a hash of `cut.json` and a list of what was not checked. Never a FAIL.
- **Review fixes for the titles work (PR 76).** A title the agent marks `unread` keeps our OCR title at that moment (`withAgent` takes the unread spans; before, the entry was dropped and the recipe lost the title while the texts said our read stays). Lines are joined as `LINE / LINE` and split only on a slash with spaces round it (`LINE_JOIN`, `LINE_SPLIT` in `kit/teardown/phrases.cjs`), so `OPEN 24/7` is one line through `Reel.md`, `titleFlips` and `shapeOf`. `npm run verify` on a `reel-fx-` file reports a frozen picture as a note, since an effect such as pause-point holds the frame on purpose (`judge(..., { fxPass })`). One limit, `MAX_TITLES` (80), for what `set_reference_titles` accepts and what the recipe keeps (the recipe kept 60). `mapPhrases` keeps words in any script (`\p{L}\p{N}`), so non-Latin speech gets phrase titles. Agent titles: the key word must sit inside one line (the recipe reader checks it against the text), and `start`, `end` and the count's numbers must be numbers (`null`, `true` and `[]` passed as 0 or 1). A `|` the OCR read in a title is cleaned before it reaches a Reel.md line, where it is the field mark. `tests/phrase-titles.test.mjs` (4 new), `tests/foldin.test.cjs`, and `tests/studio-leak.test.mjs` now also holds an older Effect and Exit pick out of a draft that copies a reference.
- **Credits.** `THIRD_PARTY.md` has "Ideas from": the three skill packs these ideas and starting numbers came from. No code of theirs is in the kit. Tests: `tests/title-effects.test.mjs` (65 render checks) and `tests/foldin.test.cjs` (12), both in `npm run check`.
- **Keep my look after a reference.** New video with Keep my look (and new footage with it on) after a Recreate, Copy the look or Influence takes your own look along, not the reference's: `kit/studio/reference.mjs` `ownLook` (a copy of the board with `undoReferenceLook` and the set-aside `prevLook` put back, the same rule as Back to my look, now `restoreLook`), used by `kit/studio/core.mjs` `freshSession` and `resetEdit`. Picks you made after the reference stay. Test: `tests/studio-leak.test.mjs`.
- **Docs.** README and `docs/studio.md` alt text under the older screenshots name today's steps (Look is Picture, Finish is Review, Footage is Start, Style is Picture, Post is a view of Render).

- **The brief per B-roll slot.** `kit/broll-brief.cjs` makes one record for every slot of the plan: `{ subject, why, line, named, shot, motion, seconds, frame, reference, avoid, serves, by }`. The words under the slot come first: your glossary and brands, then a list of common tools, sites, names written like OmniRoute, numbers, then plain nouns (what follows "the", "my", "a"). A named tool or site gives a screen recording, a number a graphic. What the reference showed is kept apart as `reference.showed`, a hint and never the subject. `serves` marks a slot whose line shares a word with the point of the video. A draft writes it into `build/plan.json` next to the slot (`kit/reel.cjs`); Studio makes it again on read with the session's words, brands and point unless someone rewrote it. `POST /api/broll/brief` (`set_broll_brief`) merges a rewrite of `subject`, `shot` and `why` into the plan. `GET /api/broll` returns `brief` (with `slot` and `text`, "Show X. Y.") on every plan slot.
- **The Asset Vault replaces the B-roll library.** `kit/studio/vault.mjs`: `vault/` in the kit by default (git ignores it), the folder from Settings, or `STUDIO_VAULT` (`STUDIO_BROLL_LIBRARY` still works). `vault.json` is the index (`items`, `trash`); each item has `items/<id>/` with `item.json`, the file under its own name, `thumb.jpg`, `frames/` (one every 2 s, up to 12, for a video) and `capture/` for a link (`og.*`, `top.png`, `full.png`, `scroll.mp4`). An item is the old clip record plus `rights` (`mine`, `licensed`, `public-page`, `reference`), `from` (`file` or `link`, with `url`, `site`, `what`), `hash` (sha256), `brand`, `text`, `status` and `note`. `kit/studio/broll.mjs` is now the reel's view of it: `load`, `get`, `search` and `rank` only see items that may be placed, so a reference only item or a link with no picture can never reach a slot, and `fill_slot` on one is refused with a plain reason.
- **Migration.** The first time the Vault opens, `library.json` in the old folder is read and every clip moves to `items/<id>/` with its id, name, tags, license, author, source link, `extId` and `usedIn`, so old sessions, stock dedupe and `credits.txt` keep working. The old index stays as `library.migrated.json`. A clip whose file is gone is left out.
- **Adding.** Files: 2 GB a file, 20 GB for the Vault (`broll.vaultGb`), the same file from an upload or a screen recording is added once (stock keeps its own id check; made cards, raw pieces and fast runs are always their own items). Links: the address is tidied (tracking parts, fragment, trailing slash; the same page with or without `www` is one link), classed as a post, a profile or a page, and saved at once with `status: "getting"` while `kit/capture.mjs` runs with the new `extras` option (full page screenshot, the share image fetched in the same browser context after the public address check, the page text, a sign in wall check). A post that is not ticked "This is mine" is `reference`. A video page keeps a still only. A sign in wall or a failed visit keeps the link with a note. `STUDIO_VAULT_CAPTURE=0` saves links without visiting them.
- **Delete with undo.** `remove` moves `items/<id>/` to `.trash/<id>/` and the record to `trash`; `restore` puts both back; `purge` drops what is older than 7 days. Slots that used the item are cleared. The delete route answers only the page (`x-studio-page`); there is no delete tool.
- **Matching, first cut (no model).** `kit/studio/vault-match.mjs` scores an item against a brief from 0 to 100 with the spec's weights: fits the brief 45 (a named thing in the name, tags, brand, title or page text, else the share of the subject's words found, else what the reference showed, for less), fits the slot 25 (shot, shape, long enough, motion), where it is from 15, fresh 10, clean 5 (flat for now). An item with none of the brief's words is not a candidate. Under 55 nothing is placed and the best guess is shown as a suggestion. `assign` gives every open slot its candidates so that no item is the first pick of two slots, slots that serve the point choosing first. The reason line is built from what matched: `From your Vault: matches "X"`, `says "X" on screen`, or `your page "X", captured last week`.
- **Sources in order.** `GET /api/broll` gives each open plan slot `need.show`, `need.suggest` and `candidates`: a Vault candidate first; with none, a stock suggestion when a Pexels or Pixabay key is set and the shot is not a screen recording (`From Pexels: "query"`), which makes no call until the user presses Use it; generation stays behind its own approval. After a draft, Vault picks over the bar are placed by `autoPick` with the reason kept in `slot.why` and `by: "Studio"`.
- **Studio page.** A Vault button in the top bar opens a liquid glass panel from any step (`kit/studio/web/src/Vault.jsx`): grid, four filters, search, Add (Upload files, Paste a link with "This is mine"), an item sheet with tags, brand, rights, where it came from and Delete with Undo, an empty state. The slot card shows the brief line, the suggestion with its reason, Use it, Swap, Skip and More (Upload, Use stock, the agent buttons, Open the Vault). Settings: "Vault folder" and "Let my agent see Vault pictures" (`broll.vaultPictures`, off by default).
- **Routes.** `GET /api/vault` (filters `q`, `kind`, `source`, `tag`, `used`, `rights`, `slot`, `limit`), `GET /api/vault/file`, `POST /api/vault/upload`, `POST /api/vault/link`, `POST /api/vault/item` (`update`, `delete`, `undo`). Bodies are checked against `kit/studio/vault-schema.mjs`, the same schemas the MCP tools list.
- **MCP.** New `add_to_vault`, `capture_link`, `search_vault` and `set_broll_brief`, with arguments checked against their schemas before anything runs. `list_broll` now returns `brief`, `candidates`, `need` (it was documented and missing) and `slots`. Tools return words about an item; a thumbnail path only when `broll.vaultPictures` is on.
- **Tests.** `tests/vault.test.mjs` (in `npm run check`): the brief rules and a rewrite, migration with credits, add, duplicates, delete with undo and purge, rights, links and captures with a stub browser, search and filters, limits, scoring, reasons and assignment, the schemas, the routes and the four tools against a running Studio. `tests/studio-vault.e2e.mjs` drives the panel and the slot card in Chrome.
- **Review fixes.** Matching looks for whole words (or their stem) and whole phrases with a word edge, so "car" no longer matches `postcard`; each item's words are split once per view (19 slots by 300 saved pages: about 1 s down to 0.1 s). `pickIdeas` no longer throws when a plan slot has no Vault pick: a plan slot takes the Vault pick or nothing, a fast run slot keeps the search by what the reference showed. `migrate` saves the index after every clip and resumes a clip whose file already moved. A capture that ends after its link was deleted writes nothing and `restore` clears a leftover folder first. `add` with `move: true` removes the file it was given when it refuses it. `addLink` on a link with no picture runs the capture again. `POST /api/broll/library` with `action: "remove"` needs the page header like `/api/vault/item`. A folder move falls back to a recursive copy when a rename is refused. Tests in `tests/vault.test.mjs` (18).
- **Not in 1.29.0.** The local picture model (SigLIP or CLIP) and search by meaning, local tags and near duplicates, face match, video download from social sites, watched folders, Refresh and Capture with me, upright scrolling recordings, capture steps, `get_vault_item`, `tag_vault_item`, `find_broll`, `request_recording`, stock ranked by picture, generation in the picking order and the shot list. See the status line of `docs/specs/broll-sourcing.md`.

## 1.28.0 (unreleased)

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.28.0.md](docs/releases/v1.28.0.md). Recreate now matches the reference's framing and colour shot by shot and draws its starburst stickers, and a new mode, Copy the look, puts a reference's style on your own script and subject.

- **Per-shot reframe (Recreate).** A teardown measures, for every shot with the speaker on screen, the face height, the eye line and the face centre with YuNet (`kit/framing.py`; `extras.json` `shots[].face`, the Reel.md shot key `face 0.16@0.32`). A Recreate measures your take the same way and sets each re-cut shot's crop (`zoom`, `punchX`, `punchY` in `cut.json`) so your face height and eye line match the reference shot (`kit/reframe.cjs`). The zoom never goes past what your source holds (2.2 on a 1920x1080 take filmed sideways, 3 on a 1080x1920 one; the log says when a shot is held there) and the face box always stays in frame. It replaces the four-step punch by shot size wherever both sides have a face. `kit/assemble.cjs` gained `punchX`. Off with `--no-reframe` or the Style line `reframe: off`.
- **Skin-protected grade match (Recreate and Copy the look).** A teardown reads each shot's colour in Lab (`shots[].lab`, the Reel.md shot key `lab`), leaving out black and short shots and the reference's own graphics. A draft fits a colour transfer from your take to it, per shot, applies only 35% of the change on skin, lowers the strength until nothing clips, and bakes a 33-point 3D LUT (`build/grade*.cube`, one for the shots that agree, `kit/grade-match.cjs`, `kit/grade_match.py`). `kit/assemble.cjs` applies it per segment (`lut`) under the finish; the finish choice is unchanged. Off with `--no-grade-match` or `grade-match: off`.
- **Starburst sticker.** The teardown tells a spiky starburst from a round seal, counts its points, reads its colour and whether it sits behind the head (with YuNet when there is no person mask), and writes `shape starburst | spikes N` on the ordinal sticker. The overlay draws a flat star with that many points in the reference's colour, the ordinal word in italic serif above it, behind your head when the mask exists. The round seal stays the default.
- **Copy the look (new mode).** `npm run look -- save <teardown dir> --name <name>` saves a reference's style as a reusable style look (`looks/local/<name>/look.json`, out of git; `kit/stylelook.cjs`): the framing mix and targets, the grade target, caption and title style, sticker and card styles, transitions, camera moves, pace, B-roll, music, sounds and loudness. `npm run reel -- --style-look <name or path> --clip <take>` puts it on any take (`kit/lookplan.cjs`): cuts at your own pauses at the look's pace (never inside a word), each shot reframed and graded to the look, its sticker on your own ordinals, numbers and keywords, its title style on your titles, its sounds at its density, its share of transitions and moves, its music by tempo or energy. Nothing from the reference's script, text or times is used.
- **Studio server.** Reference mode gains `look` (Copy the look) beside recreate, influence and off; a draft in that mode saves the reference's look as `look-<job>` and drafts with it. A saved look can be picked with no reference at all (`styleLook` in the state). `GET /api/style-looks`, `POST /api/style-looks/save`, and `styleLook` on `POST /api/state`. Agents see and set it through the existing tools.
- **Fidelity read-out.** Every Recreate and Copy the look draft writes `build/match.json` (`kit/fidelity.cjs`): an estimate from the plan of how close it is, per part (framing, grade, captions, stickers, sounds, pace), each with a score and a plain note.
- **Smaller fixes on the way.** A reframed shot is cropped before it is scaled, so a 2.2x zoom no longer blows every frame up in memory (`kit/assemble.cjs`). A sticker moved to the word you say leaves just before one of your titles comes in. `npm run verify` treats a starburst behind the head as a backdrop (only its word is measured for overlaps) and knows the mask also covers captions, stickers and cards behind the person. With no person mask on a run, a starburst meant for behind the head is kept off the face like a graphic in front (`captions.js` `personMask: false`).
- **Studio: eight steps.** Start, Cut, Picture, Text and graphics, B-roll, Music and sounds, Review, Render (`kit/studio/web/src/steps.js`; old step names and `#step=` links map to the new ones). Start holds the choice between Just my footage, Recreate a video and Copy the look, with saved looks; Review shows the match chips from `build/match.json`; Post is a view of Render. Music and sounds shows the Pacing in one line and points to Cut.
- **Studio Cut: three layers.** Raw take, Best lines and Clips, each with one line of explanation (`Cut.jsx`, `Pieces.jsx`). Clips play stitched, with the quiet the Pacing choice removes skipped on the take itself (`GET /api/cut/preview`), and a tick keeps each one. One Pacing control (Natural, Tight 0.45 s, Very tight 0.25 s; `kit/studio/pacing.mjs`) feeds `--tighten`; trimming only removes quiet between words (`kit/cutpoints.cjs` `tightenSpans`). The page says raw take, lines and clips; API, MCP and state names are unchanged.
- **The point of the video.** One optional line (`state.point`, `kit/studio/point.mjs`) on Cut and on Start with Copy the look. It lifts the score of lines that share its content words and phrases (stemmed, stop words out; `kit/studio/words.mjs`), steers the Best picks, the full edit and the clips, and marks them "Fits your point". It goes into the model and agent prompts, `get_edit`, `get_cuts` and `get_pieces`, and into drafts as `reel --goal` (`kit/goalwords.cjs`): a goal word becomes the big word of a caption or title (`kit/autocaption.mjs`). Cleared for a new video.
- **Topics.** `kit/studio/topics.mjs` replaces the single-word chips with up to 5 multiword themes: phrases of 2 to 4 words said in two or more lines, spread across the take, near duplicates merged, none when fewer than 2 hold. A connected model or agent names them when available (`topicsPrompt`, `readTopics`). Clicking one filters the lines.
- **Stage status for a reference.** `kit/teardown.mjs` and `kit/teardown.py` print `stage <id>` lines (for rea-video, which is silent, we watch the files it writes); `kit/studio/stages.mjs` turns log lines into a stage record and the server keeps its view on each queue item (`stages`: `stage`, `label`, `percent`, `list`, `failed`, `message`) in the saved state, so a reload resumes. The page shows one live line, the bar by real stages and a folded list with checks (`Stages.jsx`), on Start and in the reference view; a stopped stage says which one, with Try again.
- **Copy the look fixes.** A take already as close as the target is not zoomed (`kit/reframe.cjs` `cropFor`: the eye-line zoom no longer applies to a face within 2% of the target or bigger), and any zoom keeps the head, crown to chin, inside the frame with a 3% margin (`held: 'source' | 'headroom'` in plan.json). Stickers prefer ordinals, numbers and goal words, drop generic words, and treat the look's density as an upper bound (`kit/lookplan.cjs`).
- **A version for the Instagram Feed (4:5) and square (1:1), framed on the face.** Instagram Feed refused a 9:16 render ("only eligible for Instagram Stories"). `--export` now takes a list (`--export reels,feed45,square`): one run makes `reel-vN.mp4` (9:16), `reel-vN-feed-4x5.mp4` (1080x1350) and `reel-vN-square-1x1.mp4`. The 4:5 and 1:1 versions are no longer a fixed crop of a reel laid out for the crop. `kit/facewindow.cjs` turns the YuNet face track (`build/facezone.json`) into where the frame sits per frame: still inside a shot, a slow glide when the face really moves, an eased half-second move centred on a cut, headroom kept, the face always inside. `kit/export-frame.cjs` then makes a build of its own beside `build/` (`jobs/<job>/build-feed45/`, with `window.json`), draws the overlay again so the layout pass and the face check run for that frame, and assembles it with the picture and its person masks sliding under the frame (`cut.json` `frame`, `kit/assemble.cjs`). Full-frame b-roll is fitted to the frame, split and framed b-roll use it (`kit/split.cjs`), and a full-frame scene is drawn at its size. The timing and the sound mix are the same. `npm run export -- <job> --export feed45` makes one from the build saved beside a finished reel (`--crop` for the old plain crop); `npm run verify -- <job> --export feed45` checks it in its own build. The 9:16 reel is no longer laid out inside the 4:5 window when `feed45` or `square` is asked for. `kit/exports.cjs` gains `parseList`, `fileInfo` (the plain label for any file a render writes: "For Reels and Stories", "For the Instagram Feed") and `REFRAMED`. ## 1.12.0 (unreleased)

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.12.0.md](docs/releases/v1.12.0.md). Overlay frames render about 5x faster.

- **In-page PNG frames.** `kit/render.mjs` no longer screenshots every frame. Chrome copies the overlay canvas and encodes it to PNG on up to 3 worker threads while the page draws the next frame, and up to 3 pages draw at once (`kit/framecap.mjs`: about one page per 3 CPU threads, about 600 MB of free memory each; `RENDER_PAGES=<n>` overrides). The frame folders and names are unchanged, so assemble.cjs, fx.cjs and the rest read them as before. Stills use it too. Each page draws the reel once at 5 moments a second first and waits for the fonts and images that started loading.
- **Screenshots stay as the fallback.** `RENDER_CAPTURE=screenshot` keeps the old path. When the fast path cannot start, or fails part way (an image from a file on the canvas), render.mjs says so and that page uses screenshots from that frame on. Each run prints a `capture:` line with the frame count, time, frames per second, the path used and the page count.
- **Why PNG, not WebCodecs video.** The idea is motlab's (MIT): encode inside Chrome instead of screenshotting. Chrome's `VideoEncoder` cannot keep alpha (`alpha: 'keep'` unsupported for VP8, VP9, AV1 and H.264 in Chrome 156), and the layers need it, so the frames stay lossless PNG. See [docs/rendering.md](docs/rendering.md).
- **Measured** (`node kit/render-bench.mjs --assemble`, 30 s reel, 1080x1920, 3 layers, 2160 frames, on a cloud VM with an 8-thread Xeon, 15.6 GB and no GPU): frame capture 160.8 s to 28.2 s, render.mjs 163.1 s to 30.7 s (5.3x), render plus assemble 206.1 s to 74.1 s (2.8x). All 2160 frames pixel identical to the screenshot frames; finished reels SSIM 1.000000. Frame folders are about twice the size on disk (Chrome's PNG encoder compresses less).
- **Fix: captions over a screen recording.** A screen recording's frames go to the page as data URLs (up to 96 MB), like logos. As files they tainted the canvas, so the layout pass could not move a caption clear of the card and it was drawn over the recording.
- **Bench and tests.** `kit/render-bench.mjs` times both captures on a representative reel, compares every frame (md5, then SSIM for any that differ) and prints what WebCodecs alpha support this Chrome has. `tests/framecap.e2e.mjs` (in `npm run smoke`): three layers with a logo plate and a screen recording, one page, two pages and a forced failure part way (`RENDER_CAPTURE_FAIL_AT`), all against the screenshot frames (identical, or SSIM 0.995 or more over grey, since Chrome now and then draws a soft shadow a shade differently with either path), plus stills, and the page and worker counts.

## 1.9.0 (unreleased)

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.9.0.md](docs/releases/v1.9.0.md).

- **One cut-point module for every cut.** `kit/cutpoints.cjs` now places every cut in the kit: `clip.py` draft clips, the Studio Cut step, Lines, One clean take, Bites, the bite render and the draft's `--tighten`. Before, there were seven copies of the rules, and on real footage 42 of 390 cut edges landed inside a word.
- **Words plus the audio.** Whisper says which word a cut belongs to, and the audio says where that word really stops. The envelope is read at 32 kHz in 5 ms steps: the level of the whole band and of the 3.5 to 12 kHz band, each against a sliding room-tone floor (10th percentile over 4 s). A frame counts as speech when either band is 12 dB over the floor, so a soft "s", "f" or "th" at the end of a word is not read as silence. The envelope is cached next to the footage as `.cut.json`.
- **The rules.** A piece starts at least 70 ms (target 110 ms) before its first word sounds and ends at least 130 ms (target 180 ms) after its last sound. A dip shorter than those margins is not a word end. When a word has no pause after it to cut in, the end runs on to the next real pause (up to 2 s) instead of cutting the word off. When the pause before the next word is long enough for the tail but not for both margins, the end sits close to the next word, which is not in the piece. Whisper words over 1.5 s are re-timed to their first and last sound. Edges that ran on, sat close or found no pause are flagged (`extended`, `tight`, `sure: false`).
- **Tightening respects the audio.** `--tighten` removes only the quiet between the last sound of one word and the first sound of the next, keeping 180 ms after and 110 ms before, and skips a pause too short to hold both.
- **One shared transcript, VAD on.** `kit/studio/tx_chunks.py` and `kit/clip.py` both run faster-whisper with voice activity detection (`min_silence_duration_ms` 700, `speech_pad_ms` 300). Without it Whisper stretched a word over a 77 s stretch of room tone in one draft clip. A Studio transcript made without VAD is redone. Studio passes its own transcript to the draft (`reel.cjs --words`, new in the contract) so the draft and the Cut step cut from the same words.
- **Cut and bite renders reach the reel (bug #1).** Rendering from the Cut step or a bite now runs the reel with `--as-is` on the already-cut pieces and writes `cuts/clips.json`, so what you cut in Cut is what the reel uses. Before, the reel re-cut the source and dropped your edits.
- **clip.py.** `--lead` and `--hold` default to the shared targets and never go under 70 ms and 130 ms. Back-to-back lines with no pause to cut between them become one clip. New `--plan-only` writes `clips.json` and `clips.md` without rendering.
- **Bench.** `node kit/cut-bench.mjs take.mp4 --words take.tx.json` runs all seven paths on a take and prints, per path, mid-word cuts, lead-ins under 60 ms, tails under 120 ms, the min and median of each, and end cuts within 60 ms of the next sound (reported, not failed). It checks with `tests/cut-accuracy/measure.py`, a separate numpy and scipy measure that shares no code with the module. On Will's 5.5 min long take (7 takes joined): before, 42 of 390 edges mid-word, 76 lead-ins under 60 ms and 138 tails under 120 ms, every path failing; after, 0 of 484 mid-word, 0 short lead-ins, 0 short tails (min lead-in 105 ms, min tail 150 ms). On four of the single takes: 0 of 478 edges mid-word.
- **Tests.** `tests/cut-points.test.cjs` (envelope, fricative endings, short dips, lead-ins, run-on words, tighten) in `npm run check`; `tests/cut-accuracy/synthetic.mjs` in `npm run smoke` builds a 44 s take of synthetic speech with hiss endings and breaths and runs five paths through `measure.py`; `tests/test_clip.py` and `tests/studio-cut.test.mjs` updated for the new margins. ## 1.4.1 (unreleased)

Fixes from a long Studio session on Windows (a 13 minute recording, several pre-cut files, an Instagram reference). Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.4.1.md](docs/releases/v1.4.1.md).

- **Clips keep their first and last words.** clip.py never starts a clip after the first word begins (the first line used to lose up to 0.05 s of its onset), holds 0.35 s after the voice instead of 0.15 s, and checks a high band (above 4 kHz) so a soft consonant like the s in "this" is not cut; a piece under 0.5 s of speech joins the line before it instead of becoming its own clip. `--hold` still sets the hold.
- **Several files play in the order you gave them.** With different files (not takes of one script), clip.py keeps the file order; best-take picking runs only when most lines repeat across files. `--order files|script|auto`. Files that are already cut (short, speech from start to end, or listed in a clips.json beside them) are used whole, in order, with no re-cut; `--whole` in clip.py and `--as-is` in reel.cjs force it, and Studio's `asIs` does the same.
- **Retakes and fragments.** A line is a retake only when most of it matches another line, not one shared phrase; common openers ("I want to", "you know") do not count and a long line is never a retake of a short one. Lines under 1 s or ending on a cut-off word are marked `fragment` and left out of a draft unless nothing else is left.
- **Long recordings.** clips.json and clips.md are written as soon as the lines are found (marked `pending` while clips encode), clips encode in parallel (`--jobs`) with a hardware encoder when there is one (NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF, VideoToolbox; `--encoder` or `CLIP_ENCODER`), and clip.py prints `progress clips N/M`. The contact sheet is paged at 20 clips (clips-sheet.png, clips-sheet-2.png ...), which fixes "The filename or extension is too long" (WinError 206) on Windows with many clips.
- **A footage change from chat is applied or refused.** `{"footage": [...]}` reorders or drops files (unknown or repeated files are an error), `clips` must point at a clips.json, and a patch that changes nothing returns `noChange` over MCP and shows "that change did nothing" in the Studio chat, so an agent cannot report a change that did not happen.
- **One music bed.** The bed the draft uses is the one the music panel shows (`music.bed`); an older session's bed pick moves into it, and None means no bed at all (`--no-bed`).
- **Your words.** A glossary fixes words Whisper mishears in captions, titles and clips.json and keeps their own capitals even in an all-caps caption face: AIgent Academy, Skool (only after "on" or "in"), claude.ai, ChatGPT, Claude Code. - **Keyword logos.** When a listed word is said, its logo shows on a small white plate for about a second, at most one every 4 s and never over a title or graphic. Only logos you add (Studio: Words and brands, `--brands`, a pack's `style.brands`) or the kit's own Skool mark; nothing is downloaded.

- **yt-dlp and instaloader found after install.** Studio, reel.cjs and teardown now use the kit's own Python (.venv) when PYTHON is not set, which is where the installers put them; an **Install** button next to "not installed" runs `npm run setup:all -- --only ytdlp` (new `--only`) and updates the page.
- **A finished render is never "cancelled".** A cancel that lands after the render wrote its video keeps it and ends done; the task reads running (cancelling) until the step exits. Removing a reference only cancels a reference task.
- **Tightened pauses.** The breath kept at a tightened cut is longer (0.16 s after, 0.08 s before) and a pause under 0.2 s is left alone. Each cut pause is listed in choices.json and the Render step's Choices table with **Keep this pause** (`--keep-pauses clip@seconds`, `pace.keepPauses`).

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

- **Beat-sync and film finish.** When a music bed is set, `kit/beats.py` (librosa) writes `build/beat-grid.json` from the bed at `bedStart` for the reel length: tempo, beats, onsets, energy, and intensity-filtered drops. `"beatSync": true` (the default when a bed is set; `--no-beat-sync` to turn it off) snaps zoom punches, pack and locked-set SFX, fx hit points, and title entrances to the nearest beat or onset within 120 ms, and adds a short camera shake on strong drops (at most two per shot). Speech cuts and lip sync do not move. Optional `"finish"` on the cut or a segment applies an ffmpeg film look on the picture, before titles: `grain`, `vignette`, `chroma`, `scan-lines`, `warm`, `cool`, `teal`, `crushed`, `mono`, `bleach`.
