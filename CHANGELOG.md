## 1.10.1

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.10.1.md](docs/releases/v1.10.1.md). Fixes from Will's second real session (kit 1.7.0, a 10 minute take).

- **Pauses come from the audio (B).** Whisper stretches a word over the silence before or after it ("30" read 26.28 to 28.42 s with sound only from 28.02 s), so a pause cutter that trusts word times misses it. `kit/cutpoints.cjs` now finds pauses in the envelope first and only uses the words to name them (`pausesIn`). Re-timing also trims a word of 0.4 s or more that holds a quiet run of 0.35 s or more inside it to the side with the sound, besides the 1.5 s rule. `--tighten` and every other cut path use it. On Will's 5.5 min long take, 1.9.0's tighten left 10 pauses over 0.6 s in (10.27 s of quiet); 1.10.1 leaves none, with 0 of 506 edges mid-word, every lead-in at least 105 ms and every tail at least 150 ms.
- **A clip ends where the sound falls away (C).** The end follows the last sound and then pads, and the next line's start follows its own sound, not the next word's transcript time. 1.9.0 already placed this end right but flagged it as run on; the re-timed next word fixes the flag.
- **Short noises in pauses (D).** A short sound (up to 0.3 s) with quiet on both sides, like the 0.25 s noise between "help you." and "So join", is absorbed into the pause and cut with it (`noise` on each tightened cut). New `npm run cut` and MCP `cut_range {job, clip, from, to}` cut any stretch out of a clip by hand, applied on the next draft as a jump with short fades; `npm run cut -- noises <job>` and MCP `find_noises` list sounds of 0.08 to 0.3 s in the pauses with a ready command. The drag-to-select waveform on the Cut step waits for the Studio redesign.
- **Reels can be reopened (A).** Each export saves its build beside the video in `jobs/<job>/reel-vN.build/` (cut.json, captions.js, theme, choices, clean-up and the job's picks; videos are shared through `jobs/<job>/.media/`, base renders and masks are not kept). `npm run reopen -- <job> v3`, MCP `reopen_reel` or **Edit this reel** on Render restores it into `build/` and keeps the build it replaces as `draft-<time>.build/`. `npm run reels -- <job>` lists them.
- **Logo files no longer break the render (E).** Logos and graphic images from `captions.js`, `theme.js` and the brand plate are passed to the overlay as data URLs, so a file logo (SVG or PNG) no longer taints the canvas and stops the face check with a SecurityError. If a canvas is ever tainted, the face check now warns in `facecheck.json` instead of failing the render.
- **Draft says what it made (G).** After a draft, Studio says "Plan ready, no video yet. Press Render." With cuts kept on the Cut step, Render says so and offers **Render my N cuts**, and Draft reads **Draft whole take**.
- **Reference fallback (F).** A finish is never read from a black frame or a shot under 2 s, so a black end no longer turns every finish to mono. The Reference step says when the fallback engine read the reference and offers **Install rea-video**; `npm run setup:all` installs rea-video as an optional step (it never fails setup). The step and its tip say Influence borrows picks but does not copy the look, and Recreate does.
- **Captions (H).** A capital I inside a word ("AIgent") gets serif bars in sans caption faces, so it no longer reads as "Algent". All-caps words and words starting with I are left alone.
- **Title words lock (H).** Titles are one key word with the oneword caption look and the beat's phrase with the others (the picker did not change between 1.6 and 1.7; a different caption look gives the other kind). New lock `title-words=one|phrase` (pack key `titleWords`) pins it, so `--lock`, packs and recipes give the same titles from the same clips.
- **Tests.** Report 2 cases in `tests/cut-points.test.cjs` (4 of them fail on 1.9.0), `tests/snapshots.test.cjs`, `tests/manual-cuts.test.cjs`, `tests/logo-dataurl.e2e.mjs` (SVG and PNG logos; fails on 1.9.0 with the SecurityError) and `tests/caption-glyphs.e2e.mjs`. `tests/cut-accuracy/measure.py` adds pauses over 0.6 s to the bench. Run the bench on your own take: `python3 kit/studio/tx_chunks.py take.mp4 take.tx.json && node kit/cut-bench.mjs take.mp4 --words take.tx.json`.

## 1.9.0 (unreleased)

Release notes: [docs/releases/v1.9.0.md](docs/releases/v1.9.0.md).

- **One cut-point module for every cut.** `kit/cutpoints.cjs` now places every cut in the kit: `clip.py` draft clips, the Studio Cut step, Lines, One clean take, Bites, the bite render and the draft's `--tighten`. Before, there were seven copies of the rules, and on real footage 42 of 390 cut edges landed inside a word.
- **Words plus the audio.** Whisper says which word a cut belongs to, and the audio says where that word really stops. The envelope is read at 32 kHz in 5 ms steps: the level of the whole band and of the 3.5 to 12 kHz band, each against a sliding room-tone floor (10th percentile over 4 s). A frame counts as speech when either band is 12 dB over the floor, so a soft "s", "f" or "th" at the end of a word is not read as silence. The envelope is cached next to the footage as `.cut.json`.
- **The rules.** A piece starts at least 70 ms (target 110 ms) before its first word sounds and ends at least 130 ms (target 180 ms) after its last sound. A dip shorter than those margins is not a word end. When a word has no pause after it to cut in, the end runs on to the next real pause (up to 2 s) instead of cutting the word off. When the pause before the next word is long enough for the tail but not for both margins, the end sits close to the next word, which is not in the piece. Whisper words over 1.5 s are re-timed to their first and last sound. Edges that ran on, sat close or found no pause are flagged (`extended`, `tight`, `sure: false`).
- **Tightening respects the audio.** `--tighten` removes only the quiet between the last sound of one word and the first sound of the next, keeping 180 ms after and 110 ms before, and skips a pause too short to hold both.
- **One shared transcript, VAD on.** `kit/studio/tx_chunks.py` and `kit/clip.py` both run faster-whisper with voice activity detection (`min_silence_duration_ms` 700, `speech_pad_ms` 300). Without it Whisper stretched a word over a 77 s stretch of room tone in one draft clip. A Studio transcript made without VAD is redone. Studio passes its own transcript to the draft (`reel.cjs --words`, new in the contract) so the draft and the Cut step cut from the same words.
- **Cut and bite renders reach the reel (bug #1).** Rendering from the Cut step or a bite now runs the reel with `--as-is` on the already-cut pieces and writes `cuts/clips.json`, so what you cut in Cut is what the reel uses. Before, the reel re-cut the source and dropped your edits.
- **clip.py.** `--lead` and `--hold` default to the shared targets and never go under 70 ms and 130 ms. Back-to-back lines with no pause to cut between them become one clip. New `--plan-only` writes `clips.json` and `clips.md` without rendering.
- **Bench.** `node kit/cut-bench.mjs take.mp4 --words take.tx.json` runs all seven paths on a take and prints, per path, mid-word cuts, lead-ins under 60 ms, tails under 120 ms, the min and median of each, and end cuts within 60 ms of the next sound (reported, not failed). It checks with `tests/cut-accuracy/measure.py`, a separate numpy and scipy measure that shares no code with the module. On Will's 5.5 min long take (7 takes joined): before, 42 of 390 edges mid-word, 76 lead-ins under 60 ms and 138 tails under 120 ms, every path failing; after, 0 of 484 mid-word, 0 short lead-ins, 0 short tails (min lead-in 105 ms, min tail 150 ms). On four of the single takes: 0 of 478 edges mid-word.
- **Tests.** `tests/cut-points.test.cjs` (envelope, fricative endings, short dips, lead-ins, run-on words, tighten) in `npm run check`; `tests/cut-accuracy/synthetic.mjs` in `npm run smoke` builds a 44 s take of synthetic speech with hiss endings and breaths and runs five paths through `measure.py`; `tests/test_clip.py` and `tests/studio-cut.test.mjs` updated for the new margins. Real footage stays off the public repo, so CI checks on synthetic speech.

- **Studio: review your edit.** Five tools built for Studio. **What to swap** lists the reference's parts with times (hook, cuts, titles, b-roll and cards, graphics, CTA) from its `Reel.md`, each mapped to picks the menu has; a time seeks the reference and **Use** picks them. **Show original** plays your reel and the reference side by side or overlaid with an opacity slider, on one clock with a start offset. **Comment on this frame** sends the Studio chat one line with the reel, time and the spot or box you marked, plus the frame saved in `jobs/<job>/comments/`; agents get it in the message's `attach`. **Clean-up pass** lists pauses, filler words and flagged retakes from the Whisper word timings for keep or remove, then redrafts from the same clips with the new `npm run reel -- --cleanup <file>` (removed spans become jump cuts). **Edit receipt** shows `choices.json` as plain sentences with times and reasons, and copies as text. Logic in `kit/studio/review.mjs`; tests in `tests/studio-review.test.mjs` (in `npm run check`) and `tests/studio-review.e2e.mjs` (Chrome, a real draft from synthetic clips; in `npm run smoke`).
- **Studio: scripts and a teleprompter pacer.** A Scripts area in the Footage step (per job, in the gitignored `jobs/.studio/scripts/<job>/`): new, edit, rename, duplicate, delete, word count and read time at a chosen WPM, `[hook]`, `[body]`, `[cta]` sections and `[pause]` marks. New MCP tools `list_scripts`, `get_script` and `write_script`; ask in the chat or press **Write script from this reference**, which sends the agent a Studio message with the teardown. The Studio chat with an API key or a local model writes and saves scripts itself; keywords mode says in one line that it needs an agent or a model. The Record now teleprompter picks a script and paces it by a timer at your WPM on the take's clock (starts after the countdown, stops on pause), highlights the current line, holds on `[pause]`, has text size and mirror text, and Space, P and arrow keys. Takes keep their script, and drafts pass it to Whisper as a hint (`TX_PROMPT` in `kit/tx.py` and `kit/clip.py`). Voice-follow pacing is not included because Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to a server.
- **Humanizer skill.** `skills/humanizer` is [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (MIT, Siqi Chen) at commit a32f244, unchanged; AGENTS.md, the aigent-studio skill and `write_script` tell agents to run it on every script unless they already have one, and the Studio model chat follows a short form of its rules.
- **README comparison.** Adds HyperFrames, with links for its reference-video plan and account sign-in, and OpenShorts and kinocut.
- **Studio: Record now.** Film takes in the Footage step with the browser's camera and mic: camera and mic pickers, a live mic level, 9:16 (default), 16:9 or 1:1 frames cropped from the camera picture, mirror preview, rule-of-thirds and head and caption guides, a 3, 2, 1 countdown, pause, a timer, an optional teleprompter with adjustable speed, and a list of takes with thumbnails to keep, unkeep, retake or discard. MediaRecorder output is sent in numbered one-second chunks while you film (retried, deduplicated, ordered), converted with FFmpeg to an H.264 and AAC mp4 with a thumbnail in `inbox/studio/<job>/takes/`, and added to the footage; the Studio chat gets "New take recorded: 42 s" for the agent, and the new MCP tool `studio_takes` lists and changes takes. Clear one-line messages for a blocked, missing or busy camera and for a page that is not on 127.0.0.1 or localhost; Studio stays on 127.0.0.1. Tests: tests/studio-record.test.mjs (chunked upload and conversion of a synthetic webm, in `npm run check`) and tests/studio-record.e2e.mjs (Chrome with a fake camera and mic, in `npm run smoke`). Not tested yet: real webcams, Safari and Firefox, Windows.

The header, step bar, chat, settings drawer, hover card and toast are frosted glass over one faint ambient light; the chat and step bar float off the edges on wide screens. Slam still lands low and rises when a title is behind. Agents offer front vs behind per title. `npm run check` and `npm run smoke` cover the default, an explicit behind title, and the auto cap.

- **Teal finish is a subtle teal-and-orange grade.** The `teal` film finish uses a low-strength colorbalance: a hint of teal only in deep shadows and background, midtones left alone so skin and fabric stay natural, and a light warm lift in the highlights. It replaces a curves pair that went purple/magenta, and a first pass that tinted hair and shirt a saturated blue. Aliases `teal-orange` and `teal_orange` still resolve to it.

Flags and `cut.json` fields you set win over the preset. `--preset auto` picks a shipped style and prints what it chose. Shipped: `aigent`, `one-word`, `hormozi-bold`, `karaoke`, `minimal-clean`, `cinematic`, `editorial`, `muse-pill`, `retro-scan-lines`, `neutral` (`kit/presets/`). Agents ask first: saved preset, customize, or let me choose.
- **One-word captions.** `--style one-word` (and `--look one-word`, or the `one-word` preset) puts exactly one word on screen at a time, punchy, in the display face, timed from the word list. While a title is up it is the one word: the title drops its partner line and the caption waits. `npm run check` and `npm run smoke` cover the new style.
- **Readable titles behind the head.** Titles now rest high (`"titleY"` in `captions.js`, default 345, the centre of the big word) so most of the word clears the head and only its lower edge can tuck behind the hair. The slam lands lower, behind the head (`"titleSlamY"`, default 600), holds, then eases up to the resting height with the mask applied every frame. `"titleRise": false` (per title or for the job) lands it at the resting height. The smaller partner words of a title now draw on the front layer, in front of the person, with a thin outline, so they are never hidden. `npm run smoke` checks the slam, the rise, the partner layer and one word on screen.

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

- **Beat-sync and film finish.** When a music bed is set, `kit/beats.py` (librosa) writes `build/beat-grid.json` from the bed at `bedStart` for the reel length: tempo, beats, onsets, energy, and intensity-filtered drops. `"beatSync": true` (the default when a bed is set; `--no-beat-sync` to turn it off) snaps zoom punches, pack and locked-set SFX, fx hit points, and title entrances to the nearest beat or onset within 120 ms, and adds a short camera shake on strong drops (at most two per shot). Speech cuts and lip sync do not move. Adapted from the author's music-video project and relicensed Apache-2.0. `npm run check` validates finish names; `npm run smoke` covers the snap rules and a finish render.
